First public source and Android APK release of PulseForge.

## Highlights

- Two 16-step acid-inspired synth voices with per-step note, octave, velocity, accent, and slide editing
- Independent slide-time and accent-decay controls
- Two original synthesized drum sections
- Four-channel mixer with mute and solo
- Eight pattern banks and project save/load
- Strudel text import/export and one-tap opening at strudel.cc

## Strudel limits

The bridge transfers notes, basic drum events, tempo, and waveform hints. It does not convert accents, slides, velocity, filter settings, or other PulseForge-specific behavior. PulseForge does not embed or distribute the Strudel engine.

## Install

Download `pulseforge-0.7.0-debug.apk`. Android may ask you to allow installation from the app you use to open the file. This is a debug build for testing, not a Play Store release.

Minimum version: Android 8.0 (API 26).
