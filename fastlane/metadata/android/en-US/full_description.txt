PulseForge is a touch-based acid groovebox for writing and playing patterns on your phone.

Features:

* Two independent monophonic acid synth voices
* Saw and square waveforms
* Per-step notes, octave, velocity, accent, and slide
* Slide-time, accent-decay, and ADSR controls
* Two synthesized drum machines, each with kick, snare, hat, and clap
* 16-step sequencing and eight pattern banks
* Four-channel mixer with level, pan, send, mute, and solo
* JSON project save and load
* Export to and import from a limited subset of Strudel pattern text
* Open exported patterns at strudel.cc for browser playback

All synth and drum sounds are generated in the app. PulseForge does not include proprietary samples or code. The Strudel engine is not embedded or distributed.
